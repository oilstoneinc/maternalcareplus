The problem

Maternal outcomes depend on continuity of care — yet records, communication, and family engagement remain fragmented.

287K+
Global maternal deaths / year (WHO)

Most are preventable with timely antenatal visits, tracked vitals, and coordinated handoffs between facilities.

For health systems

  • Paper MCH books are lost, damaged, or left at home
  • No single view when patients move between hospitals
  • Manual onboarding and poor visibility into high-risk pregnancies
  • Limited tools for partner education and engagement

For mothers & families

  • Little visibility between clinic visits
  • Lab results and advice arrive late or not at all
  • Partners excluded from structured, secure updates
  • No trusted digital channel to reach care teams

For clinicians

  • Duplicate data entry across registers
  • No real-time alerts on clinical updates
  • Hard to export data for reporting and audits
  • Chat and coordination happen on personal phones
02 — ProblemMaternalCare Plus

Our solution

One platform. Four roles. Hospital-controlled access. Mothers own their journey; partners join with consent.

Hospitals

Onboard patients, manage digital MCH records, labs, vitals, appointments, national registry search, and export — with real-time notifications.

Mothers

Mobile-first dashboard: gestational progress, clinic recommendations, care history across facilities, digital MCH book, and secure messaging.

Partners

Separate accounts via hospital invite + mother-issued access code — read-only view of progress, labs, and MCH book.

Closed-loop, invite-only care

Patients cannot self-register without a hospital. Providers cannot access without institutional approval.
